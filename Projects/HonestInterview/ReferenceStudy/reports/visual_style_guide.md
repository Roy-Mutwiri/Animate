## Shared Visual Rules

1. Heads are large and readable, but they are attached to bodies through neck/shoulder construction. The primary reference uses a simple round head, thin neck, triangular/line torso, seated hips, and long thin limbs. The head is not floating.

2. Eyes carry the joke. Eye whites are large, pupils are readable black marks, and eyelids are separate expression shapes. In `Bro was so honest.mp4` at 2.88-4.48s the interviewer reaction is mostly held posture plus changing eyelids/pupils. In `Me vs Mom Cooking_.mp4` and `Fake Studying Gone Wrong!.mp4`, extreme eyes and eyelids remodel the whole face during accents.

3. Mouth drawings are custom silhouettes. The references use open oval mouths, tooth/teeth shapes, rounded vowel holes, flat annoyed mouths, and wide scream/grin shapes. The mouth changes the cheek/jaw silhouette during strong emotion; it is not just a scaled neutral mouth.

4. Linework is bold but not doubled. Most characters use a single confident black outline with thinner internal facial details. Heavy outlines are reserved for silhouettes, hands, and extreme facial contours.

5. Limbs are thin, flexible, and readable as gesture lines. Hands are simple black mitten/dot shapes or simplified palms, but the gesture is clear. The references avoid rectangular mannequin arms except where a prop or chair blocks the limb.

6. Bodies are simple, but not blank rectangles. Seated characters have shoulders, necks, chairs, knees, and feet. Torso shapes are triangular, rounded, or cloth-like; the silhouette tells posture.

7. Backgrounds are sparse but staged. The primary room uses a window, plant, side table, chairs, and perspective floor. These objects establish space and support cuts. Other clips use pale walls, simple props, and negative space for readable action.

8. Extreme drawings depart from the model. In `That one friend in class (best animation memes).mp4` around 14-20s and `Me vs Mom Cooking_.mp4` around 8s, faces stretch, eyes swell, mouths open beyond neutral anatomy, and hands cover or point across the face. The extreme pose is a new drawing, not a rig-only scale.

Comedy mechanics:
- Setup: simple staging and a clear question.
- Interruption: abrupt timing change, often a cut or sudden pose.
- Hold: the listener's reaction is allowed to breathe.
- Payoff: the objecting line lands after the audience has already read the reaction.

## Recommended Coherent Direction

Use the `Bro was so honest.mp4` staging and timing as the base style, then borrow the larger eyes/extreme expression language from the short meme references only for accents.

For the next HonestInterview design:
- Use neutral source identifiers until production role mapping is explicitly chosen: cap-wearing character and no-cap character.
- The newly inspected supplied frames override earlier clothing/prop-heavy briefs. Do not add a microphone to shots based on these supplied frames; no microphone is visible there.
- Character construction should be compact: rounded head, narrow tapered torso meeting the head directly, thin black line limbs, simple hands unless a specific action requires a posed hand, pelvis on cushion, thighs to knees, feet on floor, and an opaque armchair with correct overlap.
- Both characters need seated contact and clean chair/body overlap before any lip sync or performance polishing.

Avoid:
- Floating heads.
- Rectangular or broad bulky torsos as the main body design.
- Detached neck blocks.
- Thick colored thighs or elaborate finger anatomy when the reference uses simple line limbs/endpoints.
- Bar eyebrows disconnected from eyelids.
- Eyes as dark rings without readable whites/pupil aim.
- Continuous idle bobbing during the reaction beat.
