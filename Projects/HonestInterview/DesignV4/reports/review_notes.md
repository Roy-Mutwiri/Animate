## Corrections From DesignV3

- Reframed the opening into a medium seated two-shot from approximately thighs upward, reducing empty ceiling/wall space.
- Removed in-image labels, offscreen grin box, and production captions.
- Kept props away from hand silhouettes; the opening shot no longer uses the flower/table cluster near the grip.
- Replaced translucent chair/hip bands with opaque chair backs and seats behind the bodies.
- Added clearer neck/shoulder/body connections and more intentional curved torso contours.
- Reworked hands from circular spike-finger symbols into palms with thumbs and separate readable fingers.
- Corrected the microphone grip so the handle passes through the palm and fingers, with the mic head visible.
- Made the respondent listen with mouth closed and attentive eyes in the opening shot.
- Built the reaction as a close-up of head, shoulders, and part of the microphone hand.
- Changed the reaction face away from neutral: one brow is raised, the other pulls inward, eyelids are asymmetrical, eyes look toward the offscreen respondent, the mouth is compressed, and the head/upper body recoil slightly.
- Maintained consistent character identity across both shots: same cap, head shape, eye construction, skin colors, and simple pale bodies.
